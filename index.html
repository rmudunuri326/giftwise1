From profile to gift, in four steps

01

Create your profile

Share what you love, what you're working toward, and what you don't need.

02

Add an occasion, if you have one

Birthday, wedding, new baby — or skip this. Your profile works year-round for anytime gifts, thank-yous, and "just because."

03

Share it

Send a link for a specific moment, or keep your profile open for anyone, anytime.

04

They give their way

Help a goal, give an experience, support a cause, or simply surprise you — occasion or not.

Don't worry about finding the perfect gift — you already know what matters to them. Choose how you'd like to make them happy: help a goal, give time, support a cause, or let them surprise themselves.
